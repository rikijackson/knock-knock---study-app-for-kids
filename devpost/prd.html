Study Check-In (working title)

You knock on a door, pick who checks on you, and a funny animated character pops out to make sure you're studying.

How the scope grew into a product

"Pick who checks on you"→Knock on the door. A voice asks if you want to study, and your chosen character peeks out and asks the setup questions.
"The character pops out"→A door creak, then varied animations (eyes darting, slow nod, stare), never the same twice in a row.
"The character reacts"→A signature finale (mom's backflip!) plus a varied summary of check-ins, minutes and tasks.
"Coins unlock characters"→1 coin/minute, rising prices, and a demo coin stash so the soldier unlock happens on camera.

Look & feel

  • Cool colors only: blues and whites. No yellow, orange or warm tones.
  • Light and dark mode. Dark mode is a must.
  • Minimalistic but eye-catching. The door is the star.
  • Art: inspired by Netflix's Carmen Sandiego, with animated, shaded cartoon style and bold clean shapes.
  • Sound: knock (tuk tuk), door creak at check-ins, bang when the door slams. Dialogue stays in quiet speech bubbles.

Features & what proves they work

Door intro (first visit)
  • Only the door is visible on first visit
  • Knocks 1–2: knock sound, door opens a crack and shuts
  • Knock 3: character peeks out, "Yo, why are you knocking on my door?", then a short chat
  • Then the side buttons appear
Starting a session
  • Knock → darkness → "Do you want to start a study session?" Yes/No
  • No → bang, door slams
  • Yes → character picker shows all 3, with locked ones visibly locked
  • The character asks: timer/stopwatch → frequency → tasks → "Are you sure?"
Check-in frequency
  • Fixed: choose minutes, with very short options for the demo
  • Random: irregular, surprising times (e.g. 15, 35, 60 min)
Study screen & clock
  • The door slides left and shuts, and a huge clock appears right-center
  • Stopwatch counts up, timer counts down
  • Session tasks are listed under the clock
Breaks
  • Break pauses the clock and stops check-ins
  • Resume continues where you left off
Character check-ins ★
  • A creak plays, the door opens, the character appears
  • Several animations per character, never the same twice in a row
  • A varied speech line ("Are you sure you're studying?")
  • The character goes back in. No input needed.
Session end & finale ★
  • Ends when the timer hits zero, or when you press end
  • You're asked which tasks are done, and they get checked off
  • Signature funny finale (mom: backflip / brings out a dish)
  • 3+ tasks → a more enthusiastic finale
  • Varied summary: check-ins, minutes, tasks (the task line is skipped if 0)
  • Coins earned are shown and added
Tasks
  • Add tasks anytime in the Tasks panel
  • Choose tasks for each session
  • Completed tasks are marked done
Coins & marketplace
  • 1 coin per minute studied
  • Rising prices (100 → 200 → 300…)
  • Mother is free, soldier is unlockable, father is shown but stays locked
  • Start with 150 coins: soldier 100, father 200 (stays locked)
  • Can't buy what you can't afford
Stats & heat map
  • A heat map of hours studied per day
  • Simple numbers: most-studied day, average
  • Sample past data, labeled as demo data, with real sessions added on top

States & edge cases

What-ifs
  • No tasks: the session runs without them, and the finale skips the task line
  • Can't afford: the price shows, and buying is blocked
  • Locked character: visible but not selectable
  • On break: the clock is paused, no check-ins
  • Coming back later: coins, characters, to-dos and history are kept
  • Closing mid-session: the session is lost and not counted
