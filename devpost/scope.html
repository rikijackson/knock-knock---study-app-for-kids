Study Check-In (working title)

A free, clean-looking study website where a funny animated character pops out from behind a door to check if you're still studying.

The unique kernel

The character popping out from behind the door, which brings humor and surprise into studying.

Good study apps help you organize, but they have nothing fun to look forward to. Here, the fun is who checks on you and what they do.

"The funnier the characters, the more engaging it becomes. And the element of surprise is very important."

The core loop

Who it's for

Students who sit through long study sessions and burn out because studying feels like a grind. Today they use "study with me" YouTube videos, or study apps that are either paid or organized but joyless.

Why it matters

"Many students sit for long hours of study and often find themselves burnt out… this wouldn't be the case if they enjoyed the process."

Inspiration & feel

  • YouTube "study with me" videos where someone (a mother, a soldier…) checks on you every ~10 minutes
  • A study app where you unlock new furniture each session, the source of the unlock/reward feeling
  • Clean, funny, surprising, free. Not a paid, joyless productivity tool.

Now · Later · Cut

NOW The proof of concept
  • A desktop website (no installed app)
  • Choose a character (a few to start)
  • Check-in frequency: a fixed interval or random, including a very short option for the demo
  • Character pops out from behind a door with an animation
  • Break button to pause the session
  • Task list: plan, check off at the end, and the character reacts to how many were done
  • Coins earned for time studied → unlock new characters
LATER After the hackathon
  • Face getting sadder/angrier each day you skip. You call this a core feature, but it needs the app to remember you across days and is hard to show in a short video.
  • Optional timelapse recording of yourself studying (off by default)
  • Timer vs. stopwatch modes. This wasn't discussed for the PoC, so tell me if you want it in now.
CUT With reasons
  • App blocking (strict / super strict modes): a website can't block other apps. It would need a full desktop or phone app with special permissions.
  • Camera presence detection (with the "acknowledge you're here" countdown): detecting a person reliably could take the whole build.
