# Study Check-In (working title)

A free, clean-looking study website where a funny animated character pops out from behind a door to check if you're still studying.

## The Unique Kernel
**The character popping out from behind the door.** It brings humor and surprise into studying. Good study apps help you organize, "but it doesn't have any sort of fun element, something that I'm looking forward to." Here, the fun comes from who checks on you and what they do: "the funnier the characters, the more engaging it becomes. And the element of surprise is very important."

## Who It's For
Students who sit through long study sessions and burn out because studying feels like a grind, like the learner, who uses "study with me" YouTube videos to stay motivated. Today they use those videos, or study apps that are either paid or organized but joyless.

## The Core Loop
1. Pick who checks on you (mother, father, friend…), how often they show up (fixed interval or random), and your tasks for the session.
2. Start studying.
3. The character pops out from behind the door with a little animation. Take a break with the break button if you need to get up.
4. At the end, check off your tasks. The character reacts depending on how many you finished.
5. Earn coins for time studied and spend them to unlock new characters. **Why you come back:** to see what new character or surprise you unlock next.

## Inspiration & Identity
- YouTube "study with me" videos where someone (a mother, a soldier…) checks on you every ~10 minutes.
- A study app the learner used where you unlock a new piece of furniture each session, as inspiration for the unlock/reward feeling.
- Feel: **clean**, funny, surprising, free. Not a paid, joyless productivity tool.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
"Apps like these really help me to stay motivated." They couldn't find one that kept them engaged. Many students "sit for long hours of study and often find themselves burnt out… this wouldn't be the case if they enjoyed the process."

## What "Working" Looks Like
In a 1–3 minute screen recording: open the site, choose a character and settings, set a very short check-in time so it happens on camera, and start a session. The **character pops out and does its animation** (the "oh, that's cool" moment). Use the break button, then check off tasks at the end and see the character react. Coins are earned and a new character is unlocked. Repeat with 2–3 different setting combinations (different characters, different frequencies) to show the variety.

## The POC Boundary
- A desktop website (no installed app)
- Choose a character (a few to start)
- Check-in frequency: a fixed interval or random, including a very short option for the demo
- Character pops out from behind a door with an animation at check-in time
- Break button to pause the session
- Task list: plan tasks, check them off at the end, and the character reacts based on how many were done
- Coins earned for time studied, used to unlock new characters

## Later
- **Character's face getting sadder/angrier each day you skip.** The learner calls this a core feature, but it needs the app to remember you across days and is hard to show in a short video.
- Optional timelapse recording of yourself studying (off by default)
- Timer vs. stopwatch modes (not discussed for the PoC; tell me if you want it in now)

## Explicitly Cut
- **App blocking (strict / super strict modes):** a website can't block other apps on your computer. It would need a full desktop or phone app with special permissions, which is far beyond 2–4 hours.
- **Camera presence detection, with the "acknowledge you're here" countdown:** detecting a person reliably is hard enough to eat the whole build.
